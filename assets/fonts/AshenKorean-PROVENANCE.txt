Ashen Korean Regular

A display font bundled for Korean and English game UI.
Derived from the installed Debian fonts-noto-cjk NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc.
Upstream: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
The collection was inspected: face 1 is Noto Sans CJK KR Regular.
Subsetting was performed with fontTools, retaining layout features and all source glyphs in the Unicode ranges recorded in AshenKorean-PROVENANCE.json.
All 11,172 modern Hangul syllables (U+AC00–U+D7A3), Jamo, compatibility Jamo, Latin, punctuation, and available game symbols are included. Extended archaic Jamo available in the source are retained. Other languages and arbitrary CJK ideographs are not guaranteed.
The modified font was renamed Ashen Korean (including CFF and OpenType name fields).
